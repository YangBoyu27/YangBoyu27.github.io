ABOUT ME

BOYU YANG

BEIJING, CHINA

Hello, I am Boyu Yang, a designer with a background in architecture.

My practice explores the intersections of design, technology and human experience, with a particular interest in the body as a medium through which we perceive, interact with and understand the world. My work spans interaction design, industrial design, speculative design and spatial design, exploring how people engage with objects, spaces and each other, and how technology can create new forms of perception, expression and connection.

Moving between physical and digital environments, my work combines research, conceptual exploration and hands-on prototyping. I see design as a way to question familiar experiences, explore new possibilities and rethink the relationship between the body, technology and the world around us.

Thank you for visiting my website. I hope you enjoy exploring my work.
